# Reorders a proposed plan so the unproven thing is proven before any committing decision is made

## Claim

When given a set of decisions to make, Danny does not necessarily answer them.
He reorders them, so that decisions which **commit** — to a distribution path,
to an architecture, to a cost — are deferred until a decision that **produces
evidence** has been executed. Offered a choice he judges premature, he declines
the choice rather than picking the least bad option in it.

## Why this is flagged anti-stereotypical

The common pattern, and the one an assistant defaults to proposing, is to
minimise construction cost and settle structural questions early because they
constrain everything downstream. Danny did the reverse on the same day: he
**accepted** a large construction cost without negotiation and **refused** a
structural decision that cost nothing to make. Cost is not what he is
economising. Unfalsified commitment is.

## Evidence

One episode. **Below the promotion threshold of three distinct dates, and it
must stay a candidate until two further dates supply independent sightings.**
The single date carries three separate refusals, which is suggestive and is
explicitly *not* counted as three episodes.

- `05 Log/05.03 Verdict/2026-09-15-042` — mobile port decisions.

## What would falsify it

A session where Danny commits to a distribution, vendor or architecture
decision before any running artifact exists, when a cheap verification step was
available and he skipped it. Watch particularly for the case where he is under
time pressure: the claim is about disposition, and a disposition that only
holds when unhurried is a weaker claim than this one.

## Possible confound

Every observation so far comes from one project, `foolsgold`, which is his own
side project and carries no external deadline. The behaviour may belong to the
project's conditions rather than to him. **The two further episodes needed for
promotion should come from a different domain**, or the candidate generalises
from one activity — the failure this capture mode exists to prevent.
