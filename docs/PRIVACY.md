## 1. The short version

- FoolsGold runs on your Mac. It reads your mail to sort, summarise and prioritise it.
- We (the publisher) do **not** operate a server. We receive none of your mail, contacts or usage data.
- Your mail leaves your Mac only if **you** turn on a cloud AI provider or a network mail account. FoolsGold asks you before it does this, and you can switch it off at any time.

## 2. What FoolsGold processes, and where

| Data | Where it comes from | Where it is kept | Leaves your Mac? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email headers, bodies, attachments metadata | Apple Mail's local store on your Mac, or an account you connect | Local database in the FoolsGold data folder on your Mac | Only as described in section 3 |
| Priority verdicts, summaries, calendar suggestions, drafts | Generated by FoolsGold | Same local database | No |
| Settings and API keys you enter | You | Local settings file; keys encrypted at rest [TODO: move to macOS Keychain] | Keys are sent only to the provider they belong to |

Processing that stays on your device is not "collection" in the sense used by Apple's App Privacy labels.

## 3. When data is shared with third parties

1. **Cloud AI (optional, off by default).** If you choose a cloud AI provider in Settings (e.g. [TODO: list providers FoolsGold supports]), FoolsGold sends the text of the messages being analysed (sender, subject, body excerpt) to that provider over an encrypted connection to produce priorities and summaries. That provider handles the data under its own terms and privacy policy [TODO: link each]. FoolsGold shows this disclosure and asks for your explicit permission before the first request. Declining keeps FoolsGold on local AI (Ollama) or with no AI.
2. **Local AI (Ollama).** Runs on your Mac. Nothing is sent off the device.
3. **Mail accounts you connect** (e.g. Microsoft 365 / SMTP). FoolsGold talks directly to your mail provider to read or send mail you ask it to.

These providers may be located outside your country (for example in the United States). Where Korean PIPA applies, this section is the notice of overseas transfer: recipient, country, items transferred (message text), purpose (analysis you requested), and retention (per the provider's terms). You can refuse by not enabling cloud AI; the core features still work.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising or tracking.

## 4. Use of AI

FoolsGold uses AI to rank and summarise mail. AI-generated priorities, reasons and summaries are labelled in the app. They may be wrong, so check important messages yourself. (Korea AI Basic Act transparency notice.)
